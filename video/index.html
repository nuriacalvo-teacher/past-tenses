Chapter 01 · Finished actions

Simple past

verb + -ed·irregular: swam, caught

Finished actions at a specific time in the past.

ESvisité Roma el verano pasado · anoche vimos una peli

Finished time
I visited Rome last summer.
We watched a film last night.
Irregular verbs
Yesterday I swimmed in the sea.
Yesterday I swam in the sea.
did / didn’t → infinitive
She didn’t went to school.
She didn’t go to school. · Did you see the match?
be → was / were (no did)
The children were very tired.
Where were you born?
Chapter 02 · In progress in the past

Past continuous

was / were+verb-ing

An action in progress at a moment in the past, and the background of a story.

ESanoche a las ocho estaba cenando

At a moment in the past
At eight o’clock last night I was having dinner.
Background of a story
The sun was shining and the birds were singing.
you / we / they → were
They was playing football.
They were playing football.
State verbs → no continuous
I was knowing the answer.
I knew the answer.
Chapter 03 · The past before the past

Past perfect

had+past participle

An action before another past action. Had for everyone.

EScuando llegamos, la peli ya había empezado

Before another past action
When we got to the cinema, the film had already started.
Causes · by the time · already
By the time the police arrived, the thieves had escaped.
Earlier past → had
When I got home, I realised I have lost my keys.
I realised I had lost my keys.
Story in the past → had
After she has finished her homework, she went out.
After she had finished her homework, she went out.
Chapter 04 · Finished or connected to now?

Simple past or present perfect?

finished · whenvsup to now · no when

Look at the time words!

ESfui a Londres el año pasado · ¿has conocido a alguien famoso?

yesterday · last year · ago · when?
Last year my parents bought a new car.
When did you buy your phone?
ever · yet · so far · for · since
Have you ever met a famous person?
I’ve lived in Zaragoza for ten years.
Finished time → simple past
I have been to London last year.
I went to London last year.
No longer alive → simple past: Picasso painted thousands of works of art.
Chapter 05 · Which past?

Simple, continuous or perfect?

The trick: draw the timeline of the story.
long action · while → past continuous
I was having a shower…
short action · interrupts → simple past
… when the phone rang.
one after another → simple past
When I got to the party, Tom gave me a drink.
before another one → past perfect
When I got to the party, Tom had gone home.
when the phone was ringing → the short action interrupts: when the phone rang.
Tom went home, so I didn’t see him → he left before: Tom had gone home.
Chapter 06 · Top mistakes

Spot the mistake

The five mistakes that cost you points in the exam.

-eddid + verbwas / wereagoparticiple

ESrepasa siempre esto antes de entregar el examen

1 · -ed on irregular verbs
I catched the bus.
I caught the bus.
2 · A past form after did
Did you saw the match?
Did you see the match?
3 · was or were
We was very tired.
We were very tired.
4 · ago + present perfect
I have lost my keys two days ago.
I lost my keys two days ago.
5 · No participle after had
I had never be on a plane.
I had never been on a plane.
